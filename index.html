About Me

I am a solution-oriented, creative, and resource-driven professional working at the intersection of **Digital Spatial Technologies, Smart Agriculture, and Humanitarian Engineering**[cite: 133, 135, 160]. Currently serving as the **Local Coordination Lead for the DASTAA Project** (Digital and Spatial Technologies for Anticipatory Action), I manage regional collaborations, program implementation, and field-data frameworks.

With an educational foundation spanning an MS in Genetics and Plant Breeding from Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University, an ongoing **M.Sc. in GIS for Environment and Development at Jahangirnagar University**, and professional certification from the **MIT Center for Bits and Atoms (Fab Academy)**, I combine academic research with advanced hardware prototyping, IoT deployment, and spatial mapping pipelines[cite: 135].
